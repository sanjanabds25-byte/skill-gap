ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is Skill Gap?

A skill gap is the difference between the skills a person currently possesses and the skills required for a particular job or career.

Our platform helps users identify this gap and provides a pathway for improving their skills and finding suitable employment opportunities.

⚠️ Problem

  • Mismatch between skills and job requirements
  • Lack of career awareness
  • Insufficient technical and soft skills
  • Difficulty finding suitable opportunities
